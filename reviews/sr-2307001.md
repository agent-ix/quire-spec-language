Anchor and guard against the code (`activation.rs`): the delivery anchor is compared with the request anchor only
when an instance is established (`establish`: a different anchor gives an `Unavailable` instance with an `Anchor`
`Refused` fault, and a capture fact carrying another anchor gives a `Refused` capture fault). A redelivery of an
identity the ledger or the call already holds goes through the retained path, which ignores the delivery's
anchor, guard and captures and only adds the receipt. A new identity with guard false creates nothing and is
listed in `guard_false`; a retained identity ignores the guard. So "anchor (or guard) cannot stand in for
identity" is accurate and testable: a same-identity redelivery with a different anchor or a false guard leaves one
instance with the first captures kept and no fault; distinct identities with equal anchors are two instances. The
AC can fail, but row 4 varies neither, so the two members the AC now names have no row.
