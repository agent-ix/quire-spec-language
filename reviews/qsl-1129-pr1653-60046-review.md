# Review of PR 1653 (QSL-1129 = QSL-1121e), head 9b7844733

## Summary

Verdict FAIL on the strict bar, for two MEDIUM findings that are small: a refusal arm pair no test pins (FND-001, test-only fix) and a name-ambiguity case QSL admits that FR-464 refuses (FND-002, a pre-existing S3 gap that captures widen; a ticket is an acceptable disposition). No HIGH finding. The core of the change is correct: the binder level arithmetic, the guard slot order, the initializer scope, the sites and spans, and the key sensitivity all hold under an independent matrix probe and under 41 mutations of which every behavioural one is killed. The gate is green. If the lead takes FND-002 as a ticket, only FND-001 (add three refusal cases) stands between this and PASS with lows.

Method. QSpec FR-464/465/466 read at ccad669c (and the diff to QSpec main for FR-464: only the `owner_model` edge differs, unrelated). The pinned reader (quire-contract-ir 9a84e654, `protocol_body.rs`) and IR main (1221a1b) read side by side. Then a scratch probe (not pushed) ran the full level matrix and 50 adversarial programs through QSL's check and the real pinned reader, and a 41-entry mutation harness ran against the `emit::tests::protocol` (qsl-package) and `check::protocol` (qsl-semantics) suites.

(1) Level arithmetic, hand-verified against FR-464 "Level" and "Readable binders", then checked by a probe that is independent of the coder's tests. Spec: `over` is 0, the `each` trigger 1, capture i is the count of `over` and the trigger plus i (so 2+i under `each`, 1+i under `origin`); a role and a channel enter at `size(C0)` where `C0` is `over`, the trigger and every protocol capture; `finish` enters at the binders readable after the root control, which for a `sequence` that binds nothing is `C0`. QSL: `readable.len()` after the captures feeds `replication`, `ordering` and `finish_node`, which is `size(C0)` in every case. Probe: each in {origin, each}, n in 0..4 captures chained (each initializer reads the one before), each with a replicated role, a FIFO channel and a finish; and each with and without a guard. Every combination emitted, the pinned reader admitted it, and over/trigger/capture k/role binder/FIFO binder/finish binder sat at 0/1/base+k/base+n/base+n/base+n with base = 1 + [each]. The replicated-role binder and the FIFO message binder share a level (both enter at `size(C0)`, per FR-464: "`size(C0)` for a role, a channel and a compensation template"); the coder's test and the spec agree and a hasty reader might expect them to differ.

(2) Guard slot order: the coder is right and the lead's brief ("scope [over, trigger]") is not what lowering needs. FR-464 states no slot order; it states that the `protocol-activation` stage reads the activation binder only and bars `over` (vectors B6, B20, B22 of `protocol-node-vectors.json`). The slot order is internal to the checked body: `protocol_obligation.rs` binds the stage `ProtocolActivation` as `bind_pair(activation)` then `bind_input(over)`, so the guard's local slot 0 is the trigger and slot 1 is `over`; the capture stage binds `bind_input` first (slot 0 `over`, slot 1 the trigger, captures from 2). `activation_guard` maps slot 0 to the trigger and slot 1 to `over`, and `capture_records` uses the clause order. Mutation `slot 0/1 swapped` is killed by two tests (a guard reading `t.ready` binds the wrong parameter). The lead's [over, trigger] reading would have bound `t.ready` to the `over` parameter. Two mutations survive and are equivalent: swapping the `slot_names` array and replacing the `over` entry of the scope, because `over` is unreadable in a guard (S3 refuses it as a model-state read before lowering) and `slot_names` feeds diagnostics only.

(3) Initializer scope and sites. An initializer is lowered over `readable` as it stands before its own binder is pushed: `over`, the trigger and the earlier captures. A later capture, the capture itself, the trigger under `origin`, and in a guard any capture all refuse at S3, typed, before lowering (`StageRead` with `Forward`, `Forward`, `Unbound`, `Stage`), as FR-464 B6/B7 require. In the matrix probe, initializer k reads the capture before it and nothing later or itself, and initializer 0 reads `over` (and the trigger under `each`); the guard reads the trigger and none of `over` or the captures. Sites: the binder is placed at the capture's name, the initializer at its expression, the guard at its own tree; mutations that drop or re-key each of the three sites, and that set the `Site.location` of the guard or initializer to `None`, are all killed.

(4) Keys. Probe: renaming a capture, changing its type, changing its initializer, changing the guard, dropping the guard and moving `on each` to `on origin` each change the clause key; reformatting and a trailing comment do not; two protocols with equal captures yield one binder `parameter` node (name, level, type equal) and both clauses are admitted. Captures are ordered meaning: swapping two independent captures changes the key (a coder test), which FR-383-AC-12 states.

(5) Adversarial probes through the real pinned reader. Zero captures, four chained captures, a guard `true`, `false`, a compound guard, a guard absent: all admitted and levelled as above. A capture of every type family QSL can parse, as an `origin` capture and as an `each` trigger of the same type: Integer, Boolean, String, UUID, Timestamp, Option, Sequence, Set, Bag, OrderedSet, record, enum, a record with two fields, a model reference (`M::Order`, `M::Person`, `Reference<M::Order>`), `Quantity<m>`, `Int[0,5]`, `Text[0,5;nfc]`, bounded `Sequence`, `Decimal[..]`, `Rational[..]`, `Float64[..]`, `Option<Option<Integer>>`, `Option<Quantity<m>>`, `Sequence<Quantity<m>>`, `Set<Int[0,5]>`, `Option<Int[0,5]>`: all admitted by QSL and by the reader, so the admission is equal to the reader's verdict in both directions (QSL refuses no family at a capture and the reader refuses none; unlike a channel `carries`, a parameter's `bounded_domain` type is admitted). `Duration`, a map and a tuple are not in the grammar (refused or unparsed before the capture). The coder's type test omits Decimal, Rational, Float64, Set, Bag, OrderedSet and a model-owned type; they hold. Refused at S3, typed: a capture reading a later capture, itself or the trigger under `origin`; a guard reading a capture or `over`; a non-Boolean guard; a declared-type mismatch; a capture named like `over`, the trigger, an earlier capture, the finish binder, or a replicated role (Shadow / AmbiguousName); `pre()` in an initializer. Refused as not-yet-emitted: an initializer, guard or finish that calls a function or refers to a protocol (`CaptureReadsDeclaration`, `ActivationGuardReadsDeclaration`, `FinishReadsDeclaration`). Admitted but wrong: a FIFO message binder named like `over` or a capture (FND-002). Not probed: an initializer that reads model state through a model field of `over`, and a dispatch call.

Independent evidence today is therefore QSL's S3 (which does check them and is mutation-tested), the matrix probe above (written from FR-464, not from the coder's tests), and the QSpec vectors B1, B6, B7, B20, B22, B23, B26, TM13.
