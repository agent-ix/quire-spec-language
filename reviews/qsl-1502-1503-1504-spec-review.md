# spec-review of the temporal evaluator ports FR-522 to FR-527 (quire-spec-language PR 2164)

## Summary

Tickets: QSL-1502, QSL-1503, QSL-1504. One spec-review pass over `git diff origin/main...HEAD` at head 6e7d7d34fd6a030a7dcfa8eae0f4cd3013a5585f (6 new FRs, 6 new TCs, edits to FR-255, FR-327, FR-330, FR-499, FR-516, spec.md, TC-837), base origin/main 038cd83f0. Id check: FR-522 to FR-527 and TC-1317 to TC-1322 are unused on main and on all open PR heads (#2158, #2157, #2156, #2155 to #2146, #2142, #2140, #2130, #2122, #2106, #2088); #2163 is merged; no QSpec amendment collides. `quire validate` over all 19 changed files exits 0 (only the module duplicate-archetype warnings). Every successor criterion FR-525 names exists in the spec (136 ids resolved mechanically). The thirteen counters of FR-523 match FR-516's list exactly (ten carried, input_bytes/output_bytes/string_bytes as request_bytes/result_bytes/member_bytes, json_depth dropped), and their defaults equal the retired native limits (QSL-303, src/protocol_artifact/native_temporal/common.rs at 20ba521b8^) except formula_depth (64 there, 100000 here) and history_span (2^53-1 there because of the I-JSON cap, u64::MAX here), both explained. Legacy code read: src/temporal/{activation,budget,trace,protocol,mapping}.rs in full, result.rs and formula.rs by search, and the legacy tests for order keys, bindings and the ledger. Criterion-strength analysis was skipped: no Jev client on this machine (`jev` not found). The integrity, EARS and dependency analyses are SR-2164002 to SR-2164004.

Assessment of the author's five inferences. (1) TraceBinding required on Timestamped, Progressed and EvidenceRequest: sound for the evidence request, which replaces a document that asserted profile and clock; for Timestamped and Progressed it changes FR-501, FR-504 and FR-330 inputs without editing them (FND-012). Owner confirmation needed. (2) Multi-delivery rules in an `activate` function: acceptable as a design, but the ACs that route a repeated delivery through the one-delivery evidence entry cannot be tested (FND-002), no entry point that calls `activate` with several deliveries is named, and FR-516-AC-4 and TC-1311 row 4 keep the unexpressible vectors (FND-002). (3) prev as the predecessor value plus input_identity: needed to make unchanged and cross-obligation decidable; but the recursive chain conflicts with ADR-030 D-1 item 2 and a flat summary would do (FND-011). (4) Counter units follow the retired native limits and NFR-008: yes, except formula_depth, which the ADR and NFR-008 forbid as a limit (FND-001), and the new limit kind 'tick count' (FND-015); the author's formula_depth default decision hides rather than resolves the contradiction. (5) Open rows: OPEN-1 to OPEN-10 are real behaviors with no spine successor; the recommendations are the author's (see Verdict for rulings needed, and three missing rows: retained-instance table, restoration/cover in identity, mismatched trigger identity).

Owner rulings needed (FR-525 OPEN-1 to OPEN-10, each stays in legacy code until ruled, FR-525-AC-5): OPEN-1 order canonicalization (accept refusal; keep FR-501-AC-7's equal-stamp reversal rule), OPEN-2 interior history gap, OPEN-3 the /1 unauthenticated form, OPEN-4 TimedProfile, OPEN-5 latest-only correction (a caller-held prev cannot show it is the latest, so FR-052-AC-12 'non-direct' is unenforceable without a ledger), OPEN-6 control-to-clause mapping, OPEN-7 malformed/stale/cross-version trigger identity (FR-053-AC-9 text needs amending), OPEN-8 instances ceiling (NFR-008's active-instance verification disappears with it), OPEN-9 no-clamp for the temporal counters, OPEN-10 json_depth and the three byte counters. Rows missing from the map: retained-instance table across calls (FND-004), restoration state and clock cover in the result identity (FND-005), mismatched trigger identity and cross-version translation (FND-016), the i64-to-u64 and refuse-before-evaluation change of NFR-008-AC-1 (FND-006), and the formula_depth ruling (FND-001).

Not mergeable as written: two high findings (FND-001 depth limit against ADR-030, FND-002 an AC the one-delivery entry cannot carry) and the medium findings below. FR-527 (QSL-1504) has only one low finding and is mergeable on its own.
