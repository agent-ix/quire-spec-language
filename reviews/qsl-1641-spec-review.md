## Verdict

**NOT MERGEABLE as written.** Four high findings: an unsatisfiable atom-index AC that contradicts an unamended FR-515 AC, an undeclared change of atom identity for shared sub-terms, a window-aggregate scope cut the package bytes do not require, and a recursive-type refusal gated on a ticket that is already Done. Four medium gaps in what the bytes must supply and in the reader-view prerequisites follow.
