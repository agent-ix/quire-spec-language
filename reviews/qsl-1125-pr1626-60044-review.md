# Re-verify pass over PR 1626 (QSL-1125 = QSL-1121b), head ea686423a

## Summary

Verdict FAIL, one high finding. SR-60043 FND-001 to FND-005 are closed as claimed: the carries refusal is now exact against the pinned reader, relationships are a typed tracked refusal with the emission code gone, the real-reader carries tests exist and kill every reachable arm mutant, the tags are honest, main was merged and the specs agree. The quantity FIFO key guard (`ChannelKeyNotEqualityStable`) is however under-broad: it tests only that the key's root type is a quantity, and the reader refuses many more FIFO keys, so QSL still checks and emits packages the IR reader refuses (FND-001). That is the defect class FND-001 of SR-60043 named, left open for every key type but the one probed.

Method. Reader at quire-contract-ir 9a84e65 (`read_checked_package`), QSpec main ccad669c (FR-464/465/466). A scratch probe (not pushed) emitted each case through QSL's check and the reader twice, once as shipped and once with the two guards patched out, and compared verdicts. (a) Carries is exact. Reader and QSL agree on every probed case, unordered and FIFO where the key is the message. Admitted by both: Boolean, Integer, String, UUID, Timestamp, nominal enum, `Quantity<m>` (unordered), Option<Boolean>, Option<Int[0,5]>, Option<Quantity<m>>, Option<Color>, unbounded Sequence, Set, Bag and OrderedSet, Sequence<Int[0,5]>, Sequence<Quantity<m>>, declared records (also with bounded, quantity, Decimal, Rational, Text, Float, Option, Sequence, enum and nested record fields), and model-owned Tier (variant), Odd (variant with non-identifier cases) and Settings (record), and Option<M::Tier>, unordered. Refused by the reader with `invalid_model_binding` at `arguments/2/value/target` and by QSL as `ChannelCarriesBounded`: `Int[0,5]`, `Text[0,8;nfc]`, `Decimal[..]`, `Rational[..]`, `Float64[nearest-even]`, bounded Sequence and Set. `carries M::Account` (an object) is refused typed earlier (`ChannelCarriesObject`).
