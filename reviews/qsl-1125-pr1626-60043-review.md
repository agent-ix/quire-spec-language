# Reviewer pass over PR 1626 (QSL-1125 = QSL-1121b)

## Summary

Verdict FAIL (fix inside the PR, then re-verify). The emitted roles, channels and queues are correct against the real IR reader and QSpec FR-464/465, the gate is green and the mutation pass kills every emission mutant but one equivalent. Three things are not acceptable as they stand: the new `ChannelCarries` refusal is over-broad and its stated premise is false for two of its four families (FND-001); its test coverage is two arms of eight, the same escape class as #1619 FND-001 (FND-002); and relationship emission is neither proved through a real IR document nor reachable from a real model package (FND-003), which the ticket required.

Method. IR reader read at quire-contract-ir 9a84e65 (protocol_body.rs: CLAUSE layout line 85, ROLE/CHANNEL/QUEUE layouts 108-129, `local_details_fit` 549-620 for the replication, ordering and capacity/loss groups, `check_member` 1944 for role model, binder type, channel endpoints in the same clause, `carries` kinds and FIFO binder and key, queue channel and clause, `validate_protocol_declaration_paths` 2126 for role and channel path lengths, `reference_set_is_ordered` 395 for strictly ascending sets) and QSpec FR-464 "Binders", "Level", "Readable binders" and FR-465 "Role", "Channel", "Queue" at the merged #436 checkout (ccad669c). The coder's branch was behind main (origin/main 0ad9e1c20 is not an ancestor of 182e754ac); the scratch merge is textually clean and was gated.

Adversarial probes (scratch test module, not pushed; each emitted through the real reader unless it refuses at the check).
