| FND-001 | high | FR-185-AC-8 and the new 'Operation parameters of quantity type' bullet say `d: Quantity<Time>` comes from the domain package, so no source type reference is needed. No domain package can type a parameter as a quantity. QSL's intake resolves a parameter's typeRef to a native type (UUID, Boolean, Integer, Rational, Decimal, Float32, Float64, Text, String, Timestamp) or a package node, and ScalarValueDomain has no quantity form; FR-056 states no quantity native and no QSpec text defines one. Measured on main 40f8707b: the TC-620 fixture types `d` as `Millis = Int[1, 12]` for exactly this reason (its header cites STD-113), and `{1 ms: 90, 3 ms: 7, 12 ms: 3}` on that `d` refuses value-outside-type three times. The FR-091-OQ-11(d) note repeats the claim. Fix: keep the gap open as a dependency (STD-113, or a domain-package quantity typeRef from QSpec or FR-056), and say which side would supply the type. Do not describe it as existing behaviour. Until then, AC-8 belongs in Open Questions, not the AC table. | spec/functional/FR-185-declare-random-parameters-workloads-and-rewards.md:174-183, 206 |
| FND-002 | medium | The bullet bounds 'every sum it derives from written weights', a distribution's total and a workload's total. S3 derives no workload total. Fix: say S3 SHALL compute a workload's total and why (it bounds what FR-187 later divides by), or drop the workload clause and bound FR-187's per-state sum in FR-187. ## Verdict

Not mergeable. FND-001 (high) must be fixed: the Quantity<Time> gap is stated as existing behaviour, but no domain package can supply a quantity type. FND-002 to FND-004 (medium) should be fixed or explicitly deferred. What holds: QSpec FR-322 really has no wire form for the three declarations, so an S4 refusal is the right conservative rule. The distribution half of the derived-sum bound matches the engine's inclusive 65,536-bit RATIONAL_BITS_CEILING and its per-addition cancel poll. The text reads as system behaviour, except the 'today' clause (EARS SR-13390).
