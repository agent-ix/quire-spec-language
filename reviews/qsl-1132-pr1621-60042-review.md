SR-60041 re-check.
- FND-001 (all-features E0005): fixed. The request is built by assigning `request.source`, with no irrefutable pattern; ci-all-features is green.
- FND-002 (false NotReached under quantifier or call): fixed. Probe `forall(n in M::all_nodes : present(deref(n).next) implies true)` over a->b, b gives `Refused(UnderBinder)` and truth unchanged. So do exists, a reachable binder after a true `and` operand, and `hasNext(self) implies true` (`Refused(CalledFunction)`). An implication in an operand that a recorded decision skips stays `NotReached` even next to a binder or call (`false and forall(...)` gives `[NotReached]`; `false and hasNext(self)` gives an empty `Produced`).
- FND-003 (spec-first): fixed. FR-109 gains a "Consequent source events" section and AC-11 to AC-13, TC-1269 is added, and the tests carry `#[trace("TC-1269", "FR-109-AC-n")]` tags that match what they assert.
- FND-004 (temporal silent empty, region fault): fixed. A completed temporal verdict is `Refused(Temporal)`, a pending one is `Unavailable`, both tested. A missing region is the typed `SourceRegionUnavailable`, not an InternalFault.
- FND-005 (thin test): fixed for the straight-line subset; see FND-001 below for what remains.
- FND-006 (stable index, package id, Eq): fixed. `index`, `package_id`, `PartialEq/Eq` are present and asserted against an independently compiled package source map.

Soundness probes (scratch, 25 bodies, not pushed). Evaluator semantics read from qsl-eval `Task::Short` and `Task::Branch`: the decision is the left operand or the condition, recorded only on the claim's own level. All probed states are correct against hand derivation: `(false implies true) and (true implies true)` is Skipped then Entered; `true and (false implies true)` Skipped; `false or (true implies false)` Entered; `not (false implies (true implies true))` Skipped then NotReached; antecedent-position implications (`((false implies true) implies (true implies false))` Entered, Skipped, Entered; `((true implies false) implies (true implies true))` Skipped, Entered, NotReached); `if` taken and untaken both ways with the other branch NotReached, and an implication in the `if` condition; `let x = (true implies true) in x and x` yields one event (value evaluated once) and `let ... in (x implies x)` two; chains of three implications Entered or Skipped, NotReached; `(true or (true implies true)) and (false or (false implies true))` gives NotReached, Skipped; `not not` and repeated identical sub-expressions keep distinct indices. No false Entered or false NotReached was found. Where the walk cannot know a state it refuses.

Mutations over the PR's tests (qsl-replay lib, filter consequent_events plus the temporal test). Killed: child order not reversed, Entered/Skipped swap, implication/consequent region swap, index constant 0, truth gate removed, temporal arm to Unavailable, temporal arm to empty Produced, binder refusal deleted, call refusal deleted, `or` skip condition wrong, `and/implies` skip condition wrong, NotReached never produced, package_id constant (does not compile). Survived: `if` skip positions swapped (FND-001), the DecisionNotRecorded arm turned into a Skipped state (FND-001), the SourceRegionUnavailable code replaced (FND-001).

`SourceRegionUnavailable` has no behavioural test. This is acceptable: it guards a checked-graph invariant (every implication node has a region) that no source text can violate, the code is pinned by `refusal_codes_are_stable`, and the alternative (an unreachable fault) would be worse. Record it, do not block on it.

Consumer fit (luna IR-918 IT-008-SC-04). Nested implication, conjunction, disjunction and negation over literals or snapshot-derived antecedents produce stable indexed events including nested consequents. If the scenario's bounded assignments are authored as a `forall`/`exists` binder, or via a helper function, the run refuses typed (`consequent-events-under-binder`, `consequent-events-called-function`) until QSL-1138; that is the stated scope, not a defect.
