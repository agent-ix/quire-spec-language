## Summary

Ticket: QSL-1502 (Linear state: Done). PR: quire-spec-language#2174. Plan completion: not assessed.

AC-to-test trace of FR-522 (23 criteria) and FR-523 (19 criteria): the PR tags 20 of FR-522 and all 19 of FR-523. Untagged: FR-522-AC-13 (`evaluate_temporal_evidence` admission error), AC-14 (Finite/Lasso/Model carry no binding) and AC-20 (in-process equals reader-backed result_identity). All three need the entry point and the reader, which the PR says it does not add.

What is delivered and holds: the order and content of the clock/profile/parameter comparison on `TraceBinding::authenticate` (FR-522-AC-1..8, 12, with AC-10/11 asserted at the level of "authentication takes no meter or trace"), `AuthenticatedBinding` contents (AC-9 minus the report), the 13 ceilings, settings and defaults (FR-523-AC-1, 2, 8), counter-before-meter charging at counter refusals (AC-7, 9 partly), the tick-sum overflow (AC-16) and the iterative node count (AC-6, 17) on `ClauseWork`. Mutation checks confirm these tests fail when the code is wrong (see SR-2174001).

What is not delivered: the temporal evidence entry point and the finite-window evaluator, the report that retains `authenticated`, the real `result_identity` members (trace, progress, retained instances and ledger entry are encoded by the test itself as opaque bytes), the FR-524 ledger, FR-502 progress ledger and FR-526 relation. The PR body lists these under "Not in this PR" and says they "are listed in the ticket report". No such report exists on QSL-1502, and the ticket is Done.
