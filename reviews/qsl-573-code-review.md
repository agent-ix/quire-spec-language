# SR-13605: Code review of quire-spec-language PR #1122: QSL-573 E18a, EN-5 exact-only forms and the probabilistic product (FR-195, FR-196)

## Summary

Ticket: QSL-573 (E18a). This is the code review, with the rust-review lane folded in. Scope per the planner's ruling: untimed FR-195 (AC-1 to AC-4) and FR-196-AC-1 to AC-5; the timed product, FR-196-AC-6 to AC-9 and the canonical JCS key bytes are deferred (QSL-577).

Gates run at 14268eab1 in a fresh clone with its own `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`; I did not run `make ci`. Every gate passed, 4,874 tests passed, 0 failed, 7 ignored:

- `cargo fmt --all -- --check`
- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`
- `cargo test -p qsl-eval -p qsl-replay -p qsl-analyze -p qsl-semantics`
- root `cargo test --test it` (975 passed, 6 ignored)
- `make string-edge`
- `make arch-lint-api-surface-qsl`, `make arch-lint-canonical-encoder`, `make arch-lint-qualified-core`

I wrote a brute-force oracle in my clone (never pushed): `qsl-analyze/tests/it/rv_oracle.rs` at /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-peter-dev/be6a486a-dbff-4734-b40c-5d7654187d9c/scratchpad/rv1122/qsl. It builds random small probabilistic models (one object, `x: Int[0, 2]`, `y: Boolean`; operations `inc(b)`, `set(f)`, `rst`, `nop(b)` and a rare non-Markov `jmp(b)`, each with random preconditions, random weights, random rewards, and loose frames that give several post-states), with 24 claims each: three bounded events, `accumulate` and `steps` comparisons, an unweighted and a weighted fraction, three unbounded Reach forms, an expected reward and a long-run fraction, under the workload and under every scheduler.

- **What it does.** It never calls the builder's plan. It reads FR-187's `weighted_steps` and `actions` and the claim's three state predicates directly, enumerates every model trace to the horizon (terminal states closed by false extension for an event, extended by zero-reward stutters for a measure), and evaluates the event, the measure (activation, completion, censoring, unactivation, above-bound) and the fraction (source-position weight of the step leaving each position) on each trace.
- **What it checks.** Under a workload, the exact distribution of outcomes from forward probability propagation over the product equals the trace-tree distribution. Over every scheduler, the exact maximum and minimum probability of each outcome by backward induction over the product (choices grouped by scheduled identity, each intermediate post-state its own choice, every choice summing to 1) equals the history-dependent optimum over the model tree. For the unbounded forms, the product's states, edges, probabilities, rewards, labels, merged draws and intermediate post-states equal the model graph. Every build is checked for canonical numbering (edges grouped by source, targets numbered in first-discovery order, keys unique), for determinism (two builds equal), and for intermediate states carrying the source's model, monitor and accumulator unchanged. A `NotMarkov` refusal must match a reachable non-Markov state.
- **Undefined paths.** A second model family deletes the object (`absent-key`). Each `Undefined` path is replayed through `weighted_steps`/`actions` and must reach the named state digest, with probability equal to the product of its steps and `position` equal to its length; under a workload, `position` equals the oracle's shallowest undefined depth.
- **Result: 40 random models, 756 products, 0 mismatches** (441 semantic comparisons, 315 structural label comparisons, 154 `NotMarkov` refusals each matched, 1,016 intermediate-state edges, 591 merged edges). **40 undefined paths agree**, 0 mismatches; 14 of the 26 MDP paths pass through a draw with several post-states (FND-002). The arithmetic is exact `Rational` throughout: every DTMC state's edges sum to 1 and every MDP choice sums to 1.
- **Limits.** One object, so one `over` binding (M21 survives in the oracle too); no quantity rewards; budgets and cancellation are not compared.

Mutation testing of the PR's own tests: 36 hand-made mutants of build.rs, plan.rs, exact_probabilistic.rs, statistical/claim.rs and the S3 claim checker, each run against `qsl-analyze --test it exact_probabilistic` (plus `qsl-semantics --test it probabilistic_declarations` for the S3 mutants). 28 were killed and 8 survived.

- **28 killed:** M01 closure reject read as accept; M02 no terminal closure; M03 above-bound at `>=`; M04 no above-bound normalization; M05 horizon-active read as complete; M06 censored keeps a finite amount; M08 the activating step adds its reward; M10 fraction window `h` instead of `h + 1`; M11 no draw merging; M12 a merged edge keeps its draw; M13 merging ignores rewards; M14 a resolution edge with probability 2; M15 the intermediate advances the monitor (advance twice); M16 pending identity ignores the drawn vector; M17 the undefined path drops its last step; M19 the settled-member accept removed; M20 a DTMC edge's draw dropped; M22 no labels; M23 an MDP stutter with no rewards; M24 `ExactOnlyForm` before `EveryScheduler`; M25 `MissingConfidence` before `ExactOnlyForm`; M26 the gate misses `ExpectedReward`; M27, M28, M29 `exact_only` misses no-confidence, every-scheduler or `ExpectedReward`; M32 `always` checked as `Eventually`; M34 a measure reads its source position; M37 the cancellation ignored.
- **8 survived:** M07 an unactivated measure stays inactive at the horizon (no test reaches the horizon with `A` never held); M09 a fraction reads the post-state instead of the position it leaves; M35 a resolution advances the accumulator with zero rewards instead of the draw's; M18 and M36 `max_states`/`max_transitions` off by one (the test checks the configured value it set, not the count built); M21 the product key ignores the `over` binding (no test has two live bindings); M30 `always` and `eventually` Reach targets share one normal form; M31 the expected-reward normal form omits the reward. The oracle (below) kills M07, M09 and M35 too, so they are test gaps, not dead code.

The author's judgement calls:

1. **FR-196-AC-5's `Coin`.** The author is right that AC-5 cannot be written: I checked it with a probe in my clone. The claim as written is refused `MissingName("c")` (no `over`), and with `over (c: M::Coin)` it is refused `ProfileProhibitedOperator(Divide)`; `div` is refused `ProfileProhibitedOperator(IntegerDiv)`. The substitute (a coin deleted when `b = 0`, `absent-key`) is a faithful test of the mechanism, because the builder is cause-agnostic: it carries whatever `PredicateReading::Undefined` cause the evaluator returns. It is not a test of AC-5 as written (`division-by-zero`, `flip`, `UndefinedEvaluation{where, cause}`), and FR-202-AC-5 and FR-203-AC-5 cite that evidence. Spec fix in gap SR-13606 FND-001.
2. **FR-186 stale.** Agreed: gap SR-13606 FND-003.
3. **`not-state-predicate`.** Needs the STD-188 cause and FR-195 text (gap SR-13606 FND-006); refusing a constant goes beyond FR-195 (FND-006 here).
4. **No threshold rules for `expected`.** Agreed, and AC-2's own `<= 6/5` does not check: gap SR-13606 FND-002.
5. **No verdict for unactivated/censored.** Censored is defined (greater than every finite threshold); unactivated is not: gap SR-13606 FND-007.
6. **`ProbKey` in-memory `Ord` with the `over` binding.** Accepted for this PR: the binding is needed, because predicates are read per binding. The spec's canonical key lacks it (gap SR-13606 FND-005) and no test has two live bindings (M21, FND-008).
7. **`reward: Identifier` for `RewardRef`.** Accepted under the untimed scope; record the drift (gap SR-13606 FND-008).
8. **`steps` counts the terminal stutter.** Consistent with FR-196 ('continues on the specified zero-reward stutter steps through its horizon'); the spec should say it for `steps` (gap SR-13606 FND-007).
9. **Module name `exact_probabilistic`.** Matches FR-196's text ('QSL's `exact_probabilistic` module ... crate `qsl-analyze`'). No finding.

Other checks:

- **Exact arithmetic.** Every probability, reward and amount is a `quire_exact::Rational`; no float in the new code. DTMC edge sums and MDP choice sums are exactly 1 on every oracle product.
- **Limits and cancel.** `max_states`, `max_transitions` and `max_automaton_states` stop with the named limit, and cancellation is polled per expansion (M37 killed). The boundary itself is untested (M18, M36).
- **Determinism.** BTreeMap state index, FR-187's canonical order, breadth-first queue: two builds are equal on every oracle product.
- **Dead code and compat layers.** None. Every new variant and function has a caller; no alias or shim.
- **qsl-eval widenings.** `same_rewards` and `zero_rewards` are used by the builder (justified). `same_values` is widened and re-exported with no caller outside qsl-eval (FND-005). `WeightedStep.identity`/`random` and `PropertyAutomaton::is_settled` are used and tested.
- **The `StatePredicate` trait.** Two implementations (state-graph and probabilistic predicates) behind one evaluator: a real seam, not a one-implementation trait.

## Verdict

**Not mergeable yet: fix FND-002, FND-007 and FND-008 (medium) in this PR, and settle FND-001 (medium) either by reusing `model_check`'s product or by amending FR-196.** The rest are low.

The product itself is sound on everything the oracle could reach: 756 random products agree exactly with an independent construction, on outcome distributions, MDP optima, structure, merged draws, intermediate states and labels, with exact rational arithmetic. The S3 forms, `exact_only` and the EN-4 gate are right, and their order (`EveryScheduler`, then `ExactOnlyForm`, then `MissingConfidence`) is pinned by tests.

- **FND-001 (medium).** FR-196's SHALL-reuse is not met; the closure is duplicated from check.rs.
- **FND-002 (medium).** An MDP undefined path drops the post-state chosen at each intermediate state.
- **FND-007 (medium).** Obligation-identity distinctness of Reach targets and expected rewards is untested (M30, M31).
- **FND-008 (medium).** Five FR-196 rules have no failing test (M07, M09, M35, M18/M36, M21).
- **FND-003 to FND-006 (low).** The gate restates `exact_only`; resolution edges have no rewards; `same_values` is over-widened; a constant operand is refused beyond FR-195.
