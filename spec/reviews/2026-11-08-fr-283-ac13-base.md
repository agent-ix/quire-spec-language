## Summary

Reviewed the restated bounded-lasso case of FR-283-AC-13 and TC-766 step 7 against the base checklist. No applicable `AssuranceProfile` exists in this repository, so a direct base review was run. The old text asked for a bounded-profile clause refused "as the second clause of a request whose first clause evaluates". That cannot be built, because FR-326 gives a unit exactly one temporal profile, so every clause of a bounded-profile unit is refused over a lasso. The new text names the first selected clause of such a request. It matches the `monitor` loop, which runs clauses in request order and stops at the first refusal, and it matches the existing test `a_bounded_lasso_request_refuses_at_its_first_clause_with_no_record`. Every cross-referenced AC id in FR-283, TC-765 and TC-766 resolves. Each of FR-283-AC-1 to AC-16 is in the scope of TC-765 or TC-766 and carries at least one `#[trace]` tag. The diff adds no hash, digest, pin or tracking record.
