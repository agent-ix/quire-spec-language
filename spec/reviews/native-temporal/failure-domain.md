## Summary

The reviewed scope states the atom/constant distinction, trigger-identity rules and non-Boolean resource stops, but several source rules that keep a missing observation from becoming a Boolean are absent from it: the empty finite-window rule and its completeness authority, the authoritative-origin gate on past false extension, the incomplete-input classification of a trace that merely asserts completeness, and profile revision/digest in the identity comparison. Seventeen findings are open; none is resolved in the reviewed revision.

## Failure domains

**Trust boundaries.** The evaluator's inputs are the emitted temporal body and one caller-constructed observation trace. Every semantically load-bearing dimension of that trace — profile discriminant, clock coordinates, order keys, decision-scope progress, the completeness assertion and the authoritative origin — is asserted by the caller and only equality-checked against the declaration, never authenticated against a definition revision or digest (FND-004) or against the trace's own content (FND-003). No callback, plugin or ambient source participates, and predicate truth arrives as supplied valuations rather than evaluator-executed code, so the extension-point surface is genuinely small. The two pieces of caller-supplied logic the evaluator does run are capture initializers, constrained by FR-044-AC-6, and the activation guard, constrained by nothing (FND-006).

**Entity identity.** Result identity is keyed on profile, clock binding, limit configuration and premises, and refuses reuse on change. Obligation-instance identity is keyed on clause subject plus semantic trigger identity for the event-triggered shape only; the origin-activated shape has no key (FND-007), and one claimed identity carrying contradictory payloads has no contradiction rule (FND-008). Position identity within a trace degrades where clock coordinates tie and order keys are present on only some positions (FND-010).

**Evaluation purity.** Captures are evaluated once at the authored anchor, in source order, immutable afterwards, with ambient `self`, `result` and `pre(expr)` refused. That rule is not extended to the guard, and is not stated to survive incremental re-evaluation, eviction or admitted restoration, where re-running an initializer would satisfy every stated criterion (FND-009). Nothing bounds initializer work separately; the source's capture limit is not carried forward (FND-014).

**Topological robustness.** Termination rests on the emitter's "closed" graph and on caller-lowered ceilings with checked horizon arithmetic; acyclicity, depth and shared-node re-expansion are not constrained in this scope (FND-015). The interval algebra is finite and inclusive with `0 <= a <= b`, and unbounded forms are refused upstream.

**Outcome collapse.** The dominant failure domain is the boundary between an absent observation and a Boolean. Five of the seventeen findings — FND-001, FND-002, FND-003, FND-005 and FND-011 — are distinct paths on which an unobserved position, an unproven history boundary, an over-asserted completeness flag, a negated out-of-scope atom or a fact outside completed decision support yields a settled `true` or `false`, or erases one that was soundly settled. TC-122's Expected Results and TM-008's fourth coverage rule both claim five distinct outcomes; the controls behind that claim do not reach any of these five paths.
