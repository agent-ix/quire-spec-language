## Failure domains

**Trust boundaries.** The boundary is now declared rather than implied: FR-043 and FR-044 each open with a Semantic authority and boundary section assigning meaning to agent E, observation transport and completeness authority to agent F, and protocol results to agent B, and stating that the shared rule governs on divergence. The trace's completeness assertion, admitted order and authoritative-origin claim are explicitly trusted as supplied and retained as premises rather than silently believed. Two dimensions still enter without a declared source: the execution-origin identity (FND-019) and the progress watermark's meaning under event-position (FND-021). The definition entry authenticates identity and revision but not a digest (FND-004).

**Entity identity.** Result identity now covers profile identity and revision, clock binding name, the four declared clock parameters as retained premises, and the effective clamped ceilings, with reuse refused on any change. Instance identity covers both activation shapes, with conflicting redelivery refused rather than aliased. Position identity degrades no further: tied coordinates refuse order-sensitive operators under partial keying and under foreign authorities.

**Evaluation purity.** Captures are evaluated once per instance at the authored anchor, in source order, never re-evaluated under incremental re-evaluation, restoration or replay, and unreachable for mutation through the public API — with eviction producing an incomplete instance rather than a recomputation. The guard is evaluated once over a restricted input domain. The one purity obligation now unowned in this scope is ambient access from a capture initializer (FND-024).

**Topological robustness.** Traversal is bounded by `visits`, `depth` and `horizon` with checked i64 composition, grouping nodes traversed iteratively, and a shared node charged once per parent, so neither DAG re-expansion nor a malformed cyclic graph escapes as non-termination — each becomes a typed stop.

**Outcome collapse.** The five paths that dominated the prior review are closed. Every one of them — the empty window, the unproven history boundary, the over-asserted completeness flag, the negated out-of-scope atom and the fact outside completed decision support — now has an explicit rule, an acceptance criterion and a control, and TC-122 §3 and TC-123 §4 pin the "refused before any position was visited" claims to reported `positions` usage of zero rather than to narrative. What remains in this domain is representational rather than semantic: a result that must carry no truth has no representation in the prose contract (FND-018), and one obligation settled before a later exhaustion is governed by two rules that disagree (FND-013).
